🍕 The Pizza Shop Story

📱

You (The App)

You're hungry and want pizza! You pick up your phone to order.

🏪

Pizza Shop (The Server)

The pizza shop has lots of pizzas ready. They wait for your order!

📞 How do you talk to them?

You use special "magic words" (called HTTP Methods) to tell them what you want!

👀

GET

"Show me your menu!" (Look at pizzas)

➕

POST

"I want to order a new pizza!"

✏️

PUT

"Change my order! Add extra cheese!"

❌

DELETE

"Cancel my order please!"

🤔 What is REST?

REST stands for REpresentational State Transfer

Don't worry about the big words! It's just a fancy way of saying:

"Apps talking to each other using simple rules!" 🗣️

🎯

Simple

Easy to understand, like talking to a friend!

🌐

Universal

Works everywhere - phones, computers, tablets!

⚡

Fast

Gets things done super quickly!

🌍 Real Apps Use REST!

📱

Instagram

  • ✅ GET - See photos
  • ✅ POST - Upload photo
  • ✅ PUT - Edit caption
  • ✅ DELETE - Remove photo
🎮

Gaming Apps

  • ✅ GET - See high scores
  • ✅ POST - Save your score
  • ✅ PUT - Update profile
  • ✅ DELETE - Delete account
🛒

Shopping Apps

  • ✅ GET - Browse products
  • ✅ POST - Add to cart
  • ✅ PUT - Change quantity
  • ✅ DELETE - Remove item

🎉 Fun Facts!

🚀

Super Popular!

Almost every app on your phone uses REST to talk to servers!

🌈

Works with Everything!

Created in 2000!

Easy to Learn!

  • ✅ PUT = Change stuff
  • ✅ DELETE = Remove stuff
